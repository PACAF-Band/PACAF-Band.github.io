Band Operations Tool – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The Band Operations Tool is an internal scheduling and operations application used by members of [Your Unit]. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the general public.

Information we collect

When you choose "Sign in with Google," the app receives only your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. This is used solely to confirm that you are a listed member of the unit and to show your name in the app.

Operational information entered into the app, such as events, personnel details, leave requests, and after-action reports, is stored in Google Sheets owned and managed by the unit.

How information is used

  • To verify your identity and grant access to the app.
  • To schedule and manage unit events and related tasks.
  • To send email notifications and reminders related to your duties.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is accessible only to authorized unit members and administrators.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google sign-in data is not used for advertising or any purpose other than authenticating access to this app.

Data retention and removal

Data is retained for as long as needed for unit operations. To request a correction or removal of your information, contact the administrator below.
